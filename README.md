
## Hi there

Curiosity pulled me into software relatively late in life, and I've been building ever since.

Creating software that's genuinely useful is what keeps me motivated. A strong believer in individual agency, I see open source as one of the best ways to preserve it. Great software should earn its users by solving real problems, by forcing paywalls.

My career began maintaining fire alarm systems, an experience that shaped how I approach engineering. Straightforward, reliable systems will always be more interesting than unnecessary complexity.

Outside of coding, you'll usually find me reading random blogs from Hacker News, learning about space and engineering, hitting the gym, or planning the next project.
